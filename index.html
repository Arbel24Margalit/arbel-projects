NonoArbel

A cozy picture-logic puzzle game

Cozy picture-logic puzzles: a daily puzzle, color puzzles, Zen mode and hand-made picture packs. Works offline on your phone.

  • Daily 20×20 puzzle, a little harder as the week goes on, with a streak to keep
  • Quick play from 10×10 to 30×30, four difficulty levels
  • Color puzzles (color-blind-safe palettes included), Zen and Challenge modes
  • 30 hand-made picture packs, smart hints, light and dark themes
  • No sign-up, no ads. Progress stays on your phone
